PANNU LITTLE CORNER 🌷

HOW TO OPEN:
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open the folder "pannu_lil_corner".
3. Double-click index.html to open the website in your browser.
4. Tap the music button at the bottom to play the included song.

IMPORTANT:
Keep index.html and the assets folder together. The photo and song are loaded from assets/.
To share it online, upload the CONTENTS of this folder to a static website host. Check the host's privacy settings before publishing a personal photo.
